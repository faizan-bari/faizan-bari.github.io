Small pieces of physics you can play with. Each one runs in your browser, and each is drawn from the real equations.

A longer projectA Black Hole in a BoxAn interactive introduction to JT gravity, built up in steps: AdS₂ as a box, where the black hole hides, what matter does to the horizon, the wormhole between the two sides, and the Page curve once the box is opened. It grows and gets corrected over time.
  • Black-hole calculatorType in any mass and see the black hole it would make: its size, temperature, lifetime and glow.
  • The Page curveWatch a black hole evaporate, and see where Hawking’s calculation of the radiation’s entropy has to give way.
  • Light cones near a black holeMove toward the horizon and watch the light cones tip over, until even outgoing light falls in.
  • Orbits around a black holeLaunch a particle and watch it precess, whirl, fly by or plunge, with its effective potential beside it.
  • The Unruh effectAccelerate through empty space and find yourself in a warm bath of particles, with a horizon behind you.
  • The Penrose diagramOn the home page: tap any part of it, from the singularity to ℐ⁺, for a one-line explanation.

One more piece is hidden somewhere on the site. Try typing a certain physicist’s name.
