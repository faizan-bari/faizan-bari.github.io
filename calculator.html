The physics

How it works

  • Horizon radiusrs = 2GM / c²
  • Hawking temperatureTH = ħc³ / 8πGMkB
  • Lifetimetev ≈ 5120π G²M³ / ħc⁴

The radius grows in proportion to the mass, but the temperature falls as the mass grows: big black holes are cold and small ones are hot. A hotter black hole radiates faster, so the lifetime grows like the cube of the mass.

The lifetime is a textbook estimate. It treats the black hole as a perfect blackbody that radiates only light, and it ignores the cosmic microwave background, so read it as an order of magnitude.
