Black holes

Research

I'm interested in semiclassical gravity, quantum fields in curved spacetime and black-hole thermodynamics. Much of it starts from Hawking's discovery that a black hole has a temperature:

TH = ħκ2π c kB

κ is the surface gravity of the horizon. A black hole with the mass of the Sun sits at about 60 nanokelvin.

A black hole that radiates eventually evaporates, which raises the question of what happens to everything that fell in. The Page curve tracks the entropy of the radiation over time. If evaporation is unitary, the curve has to rise and then fall back to zero.

Current project

JT gravity and the Page curve. JT gravity is a two-dimensional model of gravity, simple enough that many calculations can be done exactly. I couple it to matter and then to a bath that collects the radiation, and use it to compute the Page curve.
